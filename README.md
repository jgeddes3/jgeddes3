## About Me

I'm an **AV & Software Engineer** in **Chicago, IL**. By day I'm the **Senior Executive Desktop Support Engineer** at **The Aspen Group (TAG)**, where I lead the company's enterprise AV modernization on Q-SYS, Crestron, Biamp and Shure and support the C-suite directly. Before TAG I worked AV at **Discover**, **Abbott**, **Kirkland & Ellis** and **Loyola University Chicago**, where I earned a B.S. in Computer Science with a minor in Philosophy.

Outside of work I build software: an encrypted habit tracker, a budgeting tool that never leaves your browser, an IT asset console that's in daily use, and a few more ideas on the way. I am always willing to try new things and work with anyone and everyone.
